# Luffy’s Origin — attempted editorial revision

> Withheld revision. Alignment did not complete.

Gold Roger amassed immense wealth, fame, and power, an achievement that sparked the onset of the Great Pirate Era.

The Red‑Hair Pirates arrived at Foosha Village. Monkey D. Luffy entered their ship and stabbed his left eye to prove his toughness. The crew celebrated his audacity with food and drink, and Luffy tried to persuade Captain Red‑Haired Shanks to let him join the crew.

Bandits led by Higuma barged into the bar and demanded alcohol. After Shanks offered a bottle, Higuma smashed it over Shanks' head. Shanks apologized, cleaned up the mess, and Luffy felt anger toward him.

Shanks attempted to grab Luffy's arm. The Red‑Hair Pirates then realized the Gomu Gomu no Mi was missing, and Shanks revealed that Luffy's body had become rubber and that he could not swim.

While the Red‑Hair Pirates set sail again, Luffy began to enjoy his rubber body.

Later, Luffy was sitting in Makino's bar when Higuma's gang returned and demanded alcohol. Makino alerted Mayor Woop Slap that Luffy had been kidnapped after a fight with the bandits.

The Red‑Hair Pirates returned; a bandit pointed a gun at Shanks, and pirate Lucky Roux shot the bandit.

Shanks declared he could tolerate insults but would never forgive anyone who harmed his friends.

Benn Beckman single‑handedly overpowered the bandits, while Higuma escaped with Luffy by throwing a smoke bomb.

Higuma took Luffy out to sea, kicked him overboard to drown him, and a Sea King known as Lord of the Coast moved to eat Higuma. Shanks arrived just in time to rescue Luffy and scare the beast away.

Shanks was relieved that Luffy survived, though Luffy was saddened by the loss of his arm during the rescue.

The Red‑Hair Pirates prepared to leave Foosha Village permanently.

Luffy decided not to try to join them, declaring his intention to form his own crew stronger than Shanks', to find the greatest treasure, and to become the Pirate King.

Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat and told him to return it once he had surpassed him. Shanks and Beckman looked forward to Luffy's future achievements.
