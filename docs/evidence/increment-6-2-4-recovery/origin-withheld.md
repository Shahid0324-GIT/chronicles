# Luffy's Origin and Devil Fruit Acquisition

**Withheld.** These are the actual paragraphs generated with the strict relationship plan. The runner reused three saved scenes and completed two more. Citations show the draft's actual note and link evidence.

Gold Roger attained enormous wealth, fame, and power.[^1] He announced that he left all his treasure at a certain place.[^2]

The Red Hair Pirates arrived at Foosha Village after completing their journey there.[^3] Monkey D. Luffy attempted to join the crew of Red-Haired Shanks, an effort that was ultimately refused because he was too young and could not swim.[^4]

Higuma entered the bar, where he broke a bottle against Red-Haired Shanks' head.[^5] Shanks apologized and cleaned up the mess, but Luffy felt angry at him.[^6] Monkey D. Luffy stormed out of the bar while Red-Haired Shanks tried to hold him back.[^7] Luffy kept walking away, and the Red Hair Pirates realized they were missing the Gomu Gomu no Mi.[^8] Red-Haired Shanks revealed his rubber body to Luffy, noting that the Devil Fruit caused an inability to swim.[^9]

After Higuma's gang demanded alcohol from Makino, she alerted the mayor to inform him of the kidnapping. Higuma planned to sell Luffy in a circus but decided to kill him instead. He kicked off Monkey D. Luffy to attempt to drown him.[^10] A Sea King came to eat Monkey D. Luffy and approached him, but Red-Haired Shanks arrived at the scene to save Luffy and scared away the beast.[^11] The Sea King ate Higuma, and Red-Haired Shanks lost his arm. Monkey D. Luffy felt sadness about Shanks' arm loss, while Red-Haired Shanks felt glad that Luffy was safe.[^12]

The Red Hair Pirates were preparing to leave Foosha Village, having completed their preparations for the journey ahead.[^13] Monkey D. Luffy decided against joining their crew, choosing instead to declare his intent to form his own group.[^14] His ambition was clear: he aimed to find the greatest treasure and become the Pirate King, a goal that drove him to reject Shanks' offer.[^15] Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat as a token of their parting, intending for it to be returned only after Luffy surpassed him in strength.[^16] Both Shanks and Beckman anticipated that Luffy would do big things, yet the Red Hair Pirates proceeded with their departure from Foosha Village.[^17]
