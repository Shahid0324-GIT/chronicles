# Limits of this proof

The focused review accepted escape to the next action as continuity within the reference account. It retained the earlier threat-to-intervention chronology and accepted a temporal link from the rescue to the immediate response. It did not verify that the Sea King ate Higuma before threatening Luffy as a separate link. The archive explicitly reports that Higuma was eaten and names Shanks as the injured person. Those reports are contextual evidence and do not on their own establish all the proposed narrative connections.
