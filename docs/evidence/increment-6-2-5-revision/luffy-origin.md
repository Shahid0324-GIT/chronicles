# Luffy's origin

> Benchmark draft. Withheld from publication.

Gold Roger attained enormous wealth, fame, and power. This achievement catalyzed a shift in the world's trajectory, leading to the entry of the Great Pirate Era.

The Red Hair Pirates arrived at Foosha Village with Monkey D. Luffy aboard. While the pirates and Luffy went to a bar to toast his craziness over food and drink, bandits led by Higuma entered and demanded alcohol.

Higuma broke a bottle over Shanks' head after he offered it. Shanks apologized and cleaned up the mess, but Luffy felt anger toward him. Instead of retreating, Shanks attempted to grab Luffy's arm.

The Red Hair Pirates realized the Gomu Gomu no Mi was missing. Shanks revealed that Luffy's body is rubber and cannot swim.

Red Hair Pirates went on another voyage, and Luffy began enjoying his rubber body. Later, while sitting in Makino's bar, Higuma's gang returned to demand alcohol. Makino alerted Mayor Woop Slap that Luffy had been kidnapped after picking a fight with them.

Shanks and the Red Hair Pirates returned; a bandit pointed a gun at Shanks, but pirate Lucky Roux shot him down. Shanks stated he could accept an insult toward him but would not forgive anyone who harms his friends. Benn Beckman overpowered the bandits singlehandedly, while Higuma escaped with Luffy in tow by throwing a smoke bomb.

Higuma took Luffy out to sea and kicked him off the boat to drown him. A Sea King known as Lord of the Coast came to eat Higuma, then moved to eat Luffy. Shanks arrived in the nick of time to save Luffy and scare the beast away.

Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat and told him to return it once he had surpassed him. Shanks and Beckman anticipated Luffy doing big things in the future.
