## Luffy's origin

Luffy asks to join Shanks' crew. Shanks declines, citing Luffy's age and swimming ability. Luffy eats a Devil Fruit the crew had stolen. The fruit gives his body rubber properties and leaves him permanently unable to swim. The archive describes the swimming limitation in both places. It leaves open how the two descriptions fit together and why Luffy chose to eat the fruit.
