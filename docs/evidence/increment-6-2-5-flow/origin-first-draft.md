Gold Roger attained enormous wealth, fame, and power, an achievement that catalyzed a shift in the world's trajectory. The Great Pirate Era entered, marking a definitive turning point where the seas became a domain for those seeking glory and fortune.

The Red Hair Pirates arrived at Foosha Village, bringing with them Monkey D. Luffy, who attempted to persuade Captain Red-Haired Shanks to let him join the crew. While the pirates and Luffy went to a bar and toasted to Luffy's craziness while eating and drinking, bandits led by Higuma entered the bar and demanded alcohol.

Higuma broke the bottle over Shanks' head after Shanks offered it, yet Shanks apologized and cleaned up the mess. Luffy felt anger toward Shanks, but Shanks attempted to grab Luffy's arm instead of retreating. The Red Hair Pirates realized the Gomu Gomu no Mi was missing, a revelation that Shanks delivered by stating that Luffy's body is rubber and cannot swim.

Red Hair Pirates went on another voyage and Luffy began enjoying his rubber body, though he was later sitting in Makino's bar when Higuma's gang returned and demanded alcohol. Makino alerted Mayor Woop Slap that Luffy had been kidnapped by the bandits after picking a fight with them, while Shanks and the Red Hair Pirates returned; a bandit pointed a gun at Shanks and pirate Lucky Roux shot the bandit.

Shanks stated he could accept an insult toward him but would not forgive anyone who harms his friends. Benn Beckman overpowered the bandits singlehandedly; Higuma escaped with Luffy in tow by throwing a smoke bomb. Higuma took Luffy out to sea and kicked him off the boat to drown him, while a Sea King known as Lord of the Coast came to eat Higuma.

The Sea King moved to eat Luffy, but Shanks arrived in the nick of time to save Luffy and scare the beast away. Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat and told him to return it once he had surpassed him, with Shanks and Beckman anticipating Luffy doing big things in the future.
