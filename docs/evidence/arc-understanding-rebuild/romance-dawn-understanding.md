# Romance Dawn · Arc Understanding Document

Grounding status: **partial**. Withheld generated paragraphs: 2.

Shanks entrusted Luffy with his iconic straw hat, promising that Luffy should return it once he had surpassed his mentor.[^1] After consuming the Gomu Gomu no Mi, Luffy's body transformed into rubber, a change that permanently removed his ability to swim.[^2]

Luffy's encounter with the bandit Higuma led to his kidnapping and an attempted drowning, during which a Sea King threatened him, but Shanks arrived in time to drive the creature away and rescue Luffy.[^3]

When Luffy asked to join the Red Hair Pirates, Shanks declined, citing Luffy's youth and his inability to swim as reasons.[^4] Undeterred, Luffy proclaimed his intention to form his own crew, surpass Shanks, and ultimately claim the greatest treasure.[^5]

Luffy and the aspiring Marine Koby arrived at a naval base ruled by the corrupt captain Axe‑Hand Morgan, setting the stage for a confrontation with the base's authorities.[^6]
