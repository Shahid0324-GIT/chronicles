# Luffy's Origin and Devil Fruit Acquisition

**Rejected draft. Chronicles generated these paragraphs. Editorial inspection found factual and originality defects, and the benchmark ended before grounding review.**

Gold Roger, the Pirate King, had attained enormous wealth, fame, and power. Before his execution, he announced to the crowd that he had left all his treasure at a certain place that could only be discovered by scouring the entire world, causing the world to enter the Great Pirate Era.[^1]

Eleven years later, the Red Hair Pirates came to a small port village named Foosha Village. A year after their arrival, a young boy named Monkey D. Luffy approached their ship and proved his toughness by stabbing his face under his left eye. The pirates and Luffy then went to the bar, where the pirates toasted to Luffy's craziness while eating and drinking. Luffy tried to convince the captain, Red-Haired Shanks, to let him join the crew, but Shanks gently refused because Luffy was too young and unable to swim.[^2]

A group of mountain bandits led by Higuma entered the bar and demanded alcohol. Bartender Makino told them the pirates had already drunk all the alcohol, and when Shanks offered Higuma the last bottle, Higuma smashed it over Shanks' head, unafraid despite his Beli8,000,000 bounty. Shanks apologized and cleaned up the mess, after which Higuma broke more dishes before leaving with his gang. The Red Hair Pirates laughed afterwards, but Luffy was angry at Shanks for not standing up for himself. Luffy stormed out of the bar; when Shanks tried to grab his arm, Luffy continued walking as his arm stretched like rubber. The Red Hair Pirates then realized the Devil Fruit they had stolen, the Gomu Gomu no Mi, was no longer in its chest, and Luffy confirmed he had eaten it for dessert. Shanks revealed that Luffy's body was now made of rubber due to eating the Devil Fruit, and that he would be unable to swim for the rest of his life.[^3]

Later, the Red Hair Pirates departed on another voyage, and Luffy began enjoying his new rubber body. One day, while Luffy was sitting in Makino's bar, Higuma's gang returned and demanded alcohol. Makino alerted the mayor, Woop Slap, that Luffy had been kidnapped by the bandits after picking a fight with them. Higuma initially planned to sell Luffy to a circus because of his strange body, but then decided to kill him for angering him. Shanks and the Red Hair Pirates returned; when a bandit pointed a gun at Shanks, the pirate Lucky Roux shot the bandit. Shanks stated that he could accept an insult toward him, but would not forgive anyone who harms his friends. First mate Benn Beckman overpowered the bandits singlehandedly, though Higuma escaped with Luffy by throwing a smoke bomb. Higuma took Luffy out to sea and kicked him off the boat to drown him, and as Luffy struggled, a Sea King known as Lord of the Coast came to eat Higuma. The Sea King then moved to eat Luffy, but Shanks arrived in time to save Luffy and scare the beast away. Shanks was glad Luffy was okay, but Luffy was saddened that he had lost his arm in the process of saving him.[^4]

Later, the Red Hair Pirates prepared to leave Foosha Village permanently. Luffy decided not to join them and declared he would form his own crew stronger than Shanks', find the greatest treasure, and become the Pirate King. To honor Luffy's promise, Shanks gave him his straw hat and told him to return it once he had surpassed him. As the Red Hair Pirates set sail, Shanks and Beckman both anticipated Luffy would achieve great things in the future. Shanks decided they had spent too much time in the village and chose to leave.[^5]
