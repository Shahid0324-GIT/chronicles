# Luffy’s Origin — frozen-evidence draft

Gold Roger attained enormous wealth, fame, and power, an achievement that marked the beginning of a new era. The world entered the Great Pirate Era following this rise.

The Red Hair Pirates arrived at Foosha Village, where Monkey D. Luffy visited their ship and stabbed his left eye to prove his toughness. The pirates and Luffy went to a bar and toasted to Luffy's craziness while eating and drinking.

Luffy attempted to persuade Captain Red-Haired Shanks to let him join the crew.

Bandits led by Higuma entered the bar and demanded alcohol, prompting Higuma to break a bottle over Shanks' head after Shanks offered it. Shanks apologized and cleaned up the mess, though Luffy felt anger toward Shanks for the incident.

Shanks attempted to grab Luffy's arm, but the Red Hair Pirates realized the Gomu Gomu no Mi was missing. Shanks revealed that Luffy's body is rubber and cannot swim.

The Red Hair Pirates went on another voyage and Luffy began enjoying his rubber body. Luffy was sitting in Makino's bar when Higuma's gang returned and demanded alcohol, leading Makino to alert Mayor Woop Slap that Luffy had been kidnapped by the bandits after picking a fight with them.

Shanks and the Red Hair Pirates returned; a bandit pointed a gun at Shanks and pirate Lucky Roux shot the bandit. Shanks stated he could accept an insult toward him but would not forgive anyone who harms his friends. Benn Beckman overpowered the bandits singlehandedly, while Higuma escaped with Luffy in tow by throwing a smoke bomb.

Higuma took Luffy out to sea and kicked him off the boat to drown him; a Sea King known as Lord of the Coast came to eat Higuma. The Sea King moved to eat Luffy, but Shanks arrived in the nick of time to save Luffy and scare the beast away.

Shanks was glad Luffy was okay, though Luffy was saddened that he had lost his arm in the process of saving him.

The Red Hair Pirates were preparing to leave Foosha Village for good. Luffy decided that he would not try to join them and declared that he would form his own crew that would be stronger than Shanks', find the greatest treasure, and become the Pirate. Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat and told him to return it once he had surpassed him. Shanks and Beckman anticipated Luffy doing big things in the future.
