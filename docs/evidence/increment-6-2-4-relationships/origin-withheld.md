# Luffy's Origin and Devil Fruit Acquisition

**Withheld narrative from the relationship-aware composition run.** Chronicles generated these paragraphs using automatic notes and the earlier set of reviewed links. The later stricter audit cleared only one rescue link and did not regenerate this draft.

Gold Roger achieved enormous wealth, fame, and power, and later announced that he had left all his treasure at a specific location that could only be discovered by scouring the entire world.[^1]

The Red Hair Pirates arrived at Foosha Village.[^2] Luffy stabbed his own face under his left eye to prove his toughness.[^3] The crew toasted to Luffy’s craziness.[^4] Luffy attempted to join Red‑Haired Shanks’ crew, but Shanks refused entry because Luffy was too young and could not swim.[^5]

Higuma entered the bar with his mountain bandits.[^6] He broke a bottle over Shanks’ head.[^7] Shanks apologized and cleaned up the mess.[^8] Luffy felt angry at Shanks.[^9] Luffy stormed out of the bar; Shanks tried to hold him, but Luffy kept walking away, revealing his rubber body, a consequence of the Devil Fruit that prevents swimming.[^10] The Red Hair Pirates realized the Gomu Gomu no Mi was missing, prompting Shanks to reveal Luffy’s new rubber body.[^11]

Luffy enjoyed his new rubber body.[^12] Higuma’s gang demanded alcohol at Makino’s bar.[^13] Makino alerted Mayor Woop Slap about Luffy’s kidnapping.[^14] Higuma planned to sell Luffy to a circus and then decided to kill him.[^15] Lucky Roux shot a bandit pointing a gun at Shanks.[^16] Shanks stated he accepted the insult directed at himself.[^17] Benn Beckman overpowered the bandits.[^18] Higuma escaped with Luffy, then kicked him off in an attempt to drown him.[^19] A Sea King approached and ate Higuma, then turned toward Luffy.[^20] Red‑Haired Shanks arrived, saved Luffy, and scared the Sea King away.[^21] Shanks lost his arm in the rescue, felt glad that Luffy was safe, while Luffy felt sadness over Shanks’ loss.[^22]

The Red Hair Pirates prepared to leave Foosha Village.[^23] Luffy decided against joining their crew and declared his intent to form his own crew to become stronger than Shanks.[^24] He aims to find the greatest treasure and become Pirate King.[^25] Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat, asking him to return it after surpassing him, and anticipated Luffy would do great things, a sentiment shared by Beckman.[^26] Shanks decided to leave Foosha Village.[^27]
