## Luffy's Origin and Acquisition of Rubber Powers

Shanks rescues Luffy from a Sea King while Higuma attempts to drown him.[^1]

Luffy asks Shanks to let him join the Red Hair Pirates.[^2] Shanks gently refuses, saying Luffy is too young and cannot swim.[^3]

Shanks gives Luffy his straw hat and tells him to return it after surpassing Shanks.[^4]

Luffy decides not to join the Red Hair Pirates and declares he will form his own crew to become stronger than Shanks and find the greatest treasure.[^5] He publicly vows to create his own crew, seek the greatest treasure, and become Pirate King.[^6]

Luffy proves his toughness by stabbing his own face under his left eye aboard the Red Hair Pirates' ship.[^7]

Luffy eats the Gomu Gomu no Mi, a Devil Fruit the Red Hair Pirates had stolen, as dessert.[^8] Eating the fruit turns his body into rubber, permanently preventing him from swimming.[^9]

Shanks reveals that Luffy's body has become rubber and that he will never be able to swim.[^10] When Shanks tries to hold Luffy's arm, it stretches like rubber, showing the effect.[^11]
