## Luffy's Origin and Acquisition of Rubber Powers

Shanks rescued Luffy from danger.[^1] Shanks gave Luffy a gift.[^2] Shanks presented Luffy with his iconic straw hat, promising its return once Luffy surpasses him.[^3] Shanks declined Luffy's request to join his crew, citing Luffy's youth and inability to swim.[^4]

Luffy consumed the Gomu Gomu no Mi, a Devil Fruit previously stolen by the Red Hair Pirates, as a dessert.[^5] The fruit transformed his body into rubber, rendering him permanently unable to swim.[^6]
