# Luffy's Origin and Devil Fruit Acquisition

**Withheld controlled draft.** Chronicles generated the prose below from human-reviewed propositions based on the frozen archive. The draft did not pass final review.

Gold Roger held the title of Pirate King and, before his execution, revealed the existence of a hidden treasure to the public, ushering in the Great Pirate Era.[^1]

The Red Hair Pirates arrived at Foosha Village where Luffy cut his face beneath his left eye to demonstrate his fitness for the crew and then asked to join Shanks' crew.[^2] Shanks refused Luffy's request, citing his youth and inability to swim.[^3]

At Makino's bar, Higuma and his mountain bandits demanded drinks, prompting Shanks to offer the remaining bottle to Higuma, who smashed it on Shanks' head.[^4] Shanks apologized and tidied the bar, while Luffy resented the lack of retaliation and walked out, during which Shanks attempted to stop him by the arm and Luffy's arm stretched as he kept walking.[^5] Later the Red Hair Pirates discovered their Gomu Gomu no Mi missing, learned that Luffy had eaten it for dessert, and Shanks explained that the fruit granted a rubber body and a permanent inability to swim.[^6]

After the Red Hair Pirates left Foosha Village, Higuma's gang returned to Makino's bar, and Makino informed the mayor that the bandits had taken Luffy after a quarrel.[^7] Lucky Roux shot a bandit aiming at Shanks, Shanks declared that insults aimed at him were tolerable but harm to friends was intolerable, and Benn Beckman overpowered the remaining bandits.[^8] Higuma escaped with captive Luffy using a smoke bomb, took Luffy offshore and threw him overboard to drown him, but the Sea King known as the Lord of the Coast ate Higuma, threatened Luffy, and was then rescued and driven away by Shanks, who lost an arm in the process.[^9] Luffy grieved over Shanks' lost arm.[^10]

The Red Hair Pirates prepared to leave Foosha Village permanently, Luffy gave up joining Shanks, declared new ambitions to form his own crew, surpass Shanks, find the treasure and become Pirate King, received the straw hat entrusted by Shanks with the promise to return once he surpasses his mentor, and the Red Hair Pirates sailed away.[^11]
