# Luffy's Origin and Devil Fruit Acquisition

**Withheld scene draft.** Chronicles generated these paragraphs from saved automatic notes. The linked archive locations show the draft’s actual citations.

Gold Roger achieved enormous wealth, fame, and power before he publicly announced that he had left all his treasure at a specific location, a secret that could only be uncovered by searching the entire world.[^1]

The Red Hair Pirates arrived in Foosha Village, where a young Monkey D. Luffy stabbed his own face under the left eye to prove his toughness, prompting the crew to toast to his craziness.[^2] Luffy then attempted to join Red‑Haired Shanks’ crew, but Shanks refused entry because Luffy was too young and could not swim.[^3]

Higuma entered a bar and broke a bottle over Shanks’ head; Shanks apologized and cleaned the mess, which made Luffy angry at Shanks, causing him to storm out of the bar.[^4] Shanks tried to hold Luffy back, but Luffy kept walking away, after which the Red Hair Pirates realized the Gomu Gomu no Mi was missing, leading Shanks to reveal that Luffy’s body had become rubber, a condition that also prevented him from swimming.[^5]

Luffy enjoyed his new rubber body while Higuma’s gang demanded alcohol; Makino alerted Mayor Woop Slap about a kidnapping, and Higuma planned to sell Luffy to a circus before deciding to kill him.[^6] Lucky Roux shot a bandit pointing a gun at Shanks, who accepted the insult, while Benn Beckman overpowered other bandits; Higuma escaped with Luffy, kicked him in an attempt to drown him, and a Sea King arrived, first approaching Luffy and then eating Higuma.[^7] Red‑Haired Shanks arrived, saved Luffy, and scared the Sea King away, but lost his own arm in the process; Luffy felt sadness at Shanks’ loss, while Shanks felt glad that Luffy was safe.[^8]

The Red Hair Pirates prepared to leave Foosha Village; Luffy decided not to join their crew, instead declaring his intent to form his own crew, find the greatest treasure, and become Pirate King.[^9] Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat, asking him to return it after surpassing Shanks, and both Shanks and Beckman anticipated Luffy would achieve great things; Shanks then decided to leave Foosha Village.[^10]
