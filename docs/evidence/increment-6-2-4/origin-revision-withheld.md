# Luffy's Origin and Devil Fruit Acquisition

**Withheld targeted revision.** The revision dropped essential parts of the rescue and did not finish review.

Gold Roger achieved enormous wealth, fame, and power before he publicly announced that he had left all his treasure at a specific location, a secret that could only be uncovered by searching the entire world.[^1]

The Red‑Hair Pirates arrived in Foosha Village, where Monkey D Luffy, eager to prove his toughness, stabbed his own face under the left eye. The crew toasted to Luffy’s craziness, and Luffy attempted to join Red‑Haired Shanks’ crew, but Shanks refused him, citing Luffy’s youth and inability to swim.[^2]

Higuma entered the bar and, in a sudden move, broke a bottle over Shanks’ head. Shanks immediately apologized and cleaned the mess, which angered Luffy. Luffy stormed out, and Shanks tried to hold him back, but Luffy kept walking away. Later, the Red‑Hair Pirates realized the Gomu Gomu no Mi was missing; Shanks revealed that Luffy’s body had become rubber, a change caused by the Devil Fruit that also prevented him from swimming.[^3]

Luffy delighted in his new rubber body. Higuma’s gang demanded alcohol, prompting Makino to alert Mayor Woop Slap about a kidnapping. Higuma then planned to sell Luffy to a circus and decided to kill him. Lucky Roux shot a bandit pointing a gun at Shanks, and Benn Beckman overpowered the remaining bandits. Higuma escaped with Luffy, only to kick him off in an attempt to drown him.[^4]

The Red‑Hair Pirates prepared to leave Foosha Village, but Luffy decided against joining their crew. He declared his intent to form his own crew, to find the greatest treasure and become Pirate King. Shanks gave Luffy his straw hat, promising its return once Luffy surpassed him, and both Shanks and Beckman anticipated Luffy’s future achievements. Shanks then decided to depart from Foosha Village.[^5]
